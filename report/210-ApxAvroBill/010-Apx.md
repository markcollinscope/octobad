## ApxRefAvroBill

The 'bill' that Avro handed over the Octopus was the start of ongoing is issues.

Octopus appeared to just accept whatever information they had been given by Avro, seeminlgy applying no due diligence on the contents of this bill.

Before this recent complaint mandated deep dive, I have not spent much time looking at energy bills. I was not 'tracking' Avro or Octopus when the handover took place so it took a little time for me to realise something was amiss with the handover bill.

When I did I raised my concern with Octoput by email 03-11-22.

## the RefAvroBill itself.

The RefAvroBill dated 29 September 2021 covered, it says, 28.08.21-26.09.21 - follows.

### Headline Figure

### Avro's Non-Sensical Bill Total

Avro somehow totalled the sum above to mean: DR £1124.93 (owed to Avro). It's worthy of note that - in retrospect at least - I had  an impression they were rather desperate for money. The payment taken from me was unnannounced and above our agreed DD amounts.

This total value given (1124.93) is the value one would get by adding the absolute (unsigned) values of all items detailed on the bill - and then throwing in a DR for good measure. It clearly makes no sense and Octopus never disagreed with my observations that it made no sense.

(Summing the absolute values of the amounts shown on the bill:

)

Clearly it makes *absolutely no sense* to sum in this way:

* a customer payment to an account

* the companies new charges to the acouunt

Whatever +/- convention is in use, these items clearly should have *the oppposite* signs.

I raised this issue with Octopus. Octopus did not disagree with my conclusion that something was very wrong.

### Accounting convention as used in bills

I was extemely busy at the time, and suprised that this matter had not been picked up by Octopus in the one year and 2 months they had by then been running the account. 

However I assumed - given the matter had been pointed out - that I could rely on a supplier of Octopus' seeming calibre to deal with the matter correctly.

Since I have been forced to undertake the deep dive necesssary for this complaint it has become clear:

* charges - money *owed by the customer to Avro* (£87.24) have an (implied) +ve (positive) sign.
* payments - money *paid by the customer to Avro* (-269.03) have an explicit -ve (negative) sign.

(see the section above - where I ask the reader to pay attention to the signs +/- used. See also the bill itself - following).

Following the +/- conventions used for payments/charges within the the bill the correct interpretation of the bill would be to use the convention of [-] for a customer credit or for customer credit balance, [+] for a customer debit (Avro charge) or being in debit. Thus:

* The starting point for the bill was that I was in credit: £768.66
* That charges of £87.24 should be deducted from that balance, and
* That my payment of £269.03 should be added to that balance

```
credit 768.66 
credit 269.03 
debit   88.24
--------------
credit 949.45
```

## Conclusion

It seems probably that the Avro account was actually in credit but that their errors - and there were very clearly errors in there - lead to Octopus flipping that into a debit value.

Even with a refund of 'part of the DR balance' (discussed elsewhere) this would leave the account over £1000 down on its true value for the whole of the last five years.
